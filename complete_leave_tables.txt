COMPLETE LEAVE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM TABLES
==========================================

=== CORE AUTHENTICATION & USER MANAGEMENT ===

1. users (Enhanced from your User table)
├── user_id (Primary Key, UUID)
├── email (Unique, VARCHAR(255))
├── password_hash (VARCHAR(255))
├── user_role_id (Foreign Key → user_roles.role_id)
├── is_active (BOOLEAN)
├── created_at (DATETIME)
├── updated_at (DATETIME)

2. user_roles (From your userroll table)
├── role_id (Primary Key, INT)
├── role_name (VARCHAR(50))
├── created_at (DATETIME)

=== ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE ===

3. departments (Enhanced from your department table)
├── department_id (Primary Key, INT)
├── department_name (VARCHAR(100))
├── total_employees (INT)
├── department_head_id (Foreign Key → employees.employee_id)
├── is_active (BOOLEAN)
├── created_at (DATETIME)
├── updated_at (DATETIME)

4. employees (Consolidated from Employee, HR Administrator, Manager)
├── employee_id (Primary Key, UUID)
├── user_id (Foreign Key → users.user_id)
├── employee_name (VARCHAR(100))
├── age (INT)
├── department_id (Foreign Key → departments.department_id)
├── manager_id (Foreign Key → employees.employee_id)
├── hire_date (DATE)
├── job_title (VARCHAR(100))
├── employment_status (ENUM: 'Active', 'Inactive', 'Terminated')
├── created_at (DATETIME)
├── updated_at (DATETIME)

├── created_at (DATETIME)
├── updated_at (DATETIME)

=== LEAVE REQUEST SYSTEM ===

├── request_group_id (Primary Key, UUID)
├── employee_id (Foreign Key → employees.employee_id)
├── total_requests (INT)
├── total_days (DECIMAL(5,2))
├── overall_status (ENUM: 'Pending', 'Approved', 'Rejected', 'Partially_Approved', 'Cancelled')
├── requested_at (DATETIME)
├── is_responded (BOOLEAN)
├── responded_at (DATETIME)
├── final_approver_id (Foreign Key → employees.employee_id)
├── comments (TEXT)
├── created_at (DATETIME)
├── updated_at (DATETIME)

10. leave_requests (Your LeaveRequest + improvements)
├── request_id (Primary Key, UUID)
├── request_group_id (Foreign Key → leave_request_groups.request_group_id)
├── leave_type_id (Foreign Key → leave_types.leave_type_id)
